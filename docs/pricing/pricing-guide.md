## FAQ

### Can I change plans anytime?
Yes! Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately with prorated billing.

### What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data is preserved for 90 days after cancellation. You can export it anytime during this period.

### Do you offer student discounts?
Yes! Students get 50% off Pro plans. Contact support with your student ID.

### Is there a setup fee?
No setup fees for any plan. You only pay the subscription cost.

### Can I pay annually for the Free plan?
The Free plan is only available as a monthly subscription.

### What counts as an API request?
Each API call to our endpoints counts as a request. Rate limits are per hour.

### Do you offer refunds for unused time?
Yes, we offer prorated refunds for annual plans cancelled early.

### Can I use LegalEdge for commercial purposes?
Yes, but Enterprise features may be required for commercial use. Contact sales for details.
