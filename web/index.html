What LegalEdge does—and doesn't do

LegalEdge retrieves a recent-record window from two official U.S. government sources. Transparent keyword patterns categorize sector topics. No machine-learned return forecasts, trade conflict allegations, probability-of-passage estimates or artificial risk scores are presented.

The feed is not a complete legislative archive; publisher availability and publish dates vary. Review the original publication before making decisions. This is a research tool, not financial or legal advice.
